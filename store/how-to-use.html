How to use PureFlow

Ad-free, distraction-free watching. Takes 30 seconds to set up.

Get started

  1. Install PureFlow from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome and click the pin next to PureFlow, so its icon is always visible.
  3. Open any video. Ads are blocked straight away. There is nothing to set up.

The popup

Click the PureFlow icon to open it.

  • On/off switch (top right): pause PureFlow any time. Turn it back on and any ad on screen is stopped at once.
  • Stats: how many ads were blocked and how much time you saved.
  • Allow ads on this channel: shows while a video is open. Switch it on to support that creator. Starts from their next video.
  • Focus: hide Shorts, comments, recommendations and end-of-video suggestions.
  • Speed: tap 1x to 3x. The video changes right away and PureFlow remembers it for the next ones. "Off" leaves speed alone.
  • Skip sponsor parts: jumps over paid segments inside videos. Off until you turn it on.
  • Add your name (bottom): type your name and press Save. The greeting uses it. It stays on your computer.
  • Reset stats (bottom): start your counters again from zero.

Good to know

  • If an ad ever slips through, PureFlow mutes it and skips it. You may see "Ad blocked, back in a sec" for a moment.
  • After PureFlow updates, the popup may say "Refresh this tab to finish updating PureFlow". Press Refresh once and it goes away.
  • Everything stays in your browser. See the privacy policy.

Something not working? Turn PureFlow off and on from the popup, or refresh the tab. Still stuck? Email YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL.
