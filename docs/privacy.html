PureFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

PureFlow is a browser extension that removes ads and distractions while you watch videos. It is built to work without collecting your data. This page explains exactly what it stores and the one optional feature that talks to an outside service.

The short version

  • No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • We (the developer) never receive any of your data.
  • We never sell or share data.

Optional: Skip sponsor parts (SponsorBlock)

This feature is off by default. If you turn it on, PureFlow asks SponsorBlock, a free community database, where the sponsored parts of a video are. To protect your privacy it sends only the first 4 characters of a SHA-256 hash of the video's id, never the video id itself, your name, or any account information. SponsorBlock returns segments for many videos that share those 4 characters, and PureFlow picks the right one inside your browser.

SponsorBlock is run by a third party with its own privacy policy. Sponsor segment data is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 from https://sponsor.ajay.app/.

Permissions and why we need them

  • Site access (all websites): to block ads and hide empty ad boxes on every website you visit, not only on YouTube. This happens inside your browser: PureFlow never reads, saves or sends the content of the pages you visit. It only uses a site's name, on your computer, to pick that site's ad-box rules.
  • declarativeNetRequest: blocks requests to ad servers using Chrome's built-in engine (on YouTube, PureFlow's own list; elsewhere, the public EasyList).
  • scripting: starts PureFlow in tabs that were already open when you installed it, and adds the ad-box hiding rules to pages.
  • activeTab: when you open the popup on a website, shows how many ads were blocked on that page.
  • storage: saves your settings and stats.

Changes to this policy

If PureFlow ever handles data differently, this page will be updated and the extension will tell you inside its popup before the change takes effect.
